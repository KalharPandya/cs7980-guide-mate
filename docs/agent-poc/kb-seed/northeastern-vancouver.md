# Northeastern University Vancouver Campus

Northeastern's Vancouver campus is one of the university's global campuses, located in
downtown Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. It focuses on graduate education in
computer science and related fields, including programs for learners transitioning
into tech (such as the Align pathway).

## The campus space
- The campus occupies three floors of its building: the 2nd floor, the 14th floor, and
  the 15th floor.
- Classroom 1526 is on the 15th floor. It is the home base of Moses the robot dog, and
  where the CS 7980 capstone demos happen.
- For help on campus, students can ask campus staff at the front desk or ask Moses,
  who can answer questions and, in the longer-term vision, physically guide visitors.

## CS 7980 and the Moses project
- CS 7980 is a computer science capstone course. The Moses robot dog project is a
  CS 7980 capstone project by Kalhar Pandya at Northeastern Vancouver.
- The project's long-term goal is a guide robot for the campus: for example, picking a
  visitor up at the front desk and guiding them to a room.
- Demos run live in classroom 1526 with students in the room and more attending online.
