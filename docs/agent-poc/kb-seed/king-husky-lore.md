# King Husky, Northeastern's Mascot: History and Lore

King Husky is the mascot of Northeastern University. The tradition began in 1927 and
continues today with a line of live Siberian Husky mascots. The current reigning live
King Husky is named Moses.

## Origin: the 1925 serum run
- The mascot tradition was inspired by the 1925 Alaska serum run, when sled dog teams
  relayed diphtheria antitoxin about 674 miles across Alaska in under a week to save the
  town of Nome.
- Northeastern's first mascot, a Siberian Husky named Sapsut, was the son of a dog that
  ran in the serum run. He was delivered by the famous musher Leonhard Seppala.

## The line of King Huskies
- King Husky I (Sapsut) debuted on March 4, 1927. Northeastern canceled classes for the
  celebration, and he received an honorary degree and the crown "King Husky". He served
  14 years, until 1941, and students once pooled money to buy him a tan leather collar
  and leash.
- King Husky II was crowned on January 12, 1942. He came from Chinook Kennels (Eva
  Seeley) and served 10 years until retiring in 1952.
- King Husky III appeared in October 1952.
- King Husky IV was crowned in 1958.
- King Husky V served from 1965 to 1970. The graduating class of 1970 funded him, with
  the playful stipulation that the dog would graduate with the class.

## Costume and statue era
- In the 1960s, a male student was elected each year to wear a husky suit at sporting
  events as Mr. Husky; Mrs. Husky was added later as a co-mascot.
- A bronze King Husky statue was installed in Ell Hall in 1962, sculpted by Adio di
  Biccari and Arcangelo Cascieri. Rubbing the statue's nose is a good-luck tradition
  for students.
- Paws, the costumed husky mascot, was introduced in fall 2003 and appears at athletic
  and campus events.

## The modern live mascot and Moses
- The live mascot tradition was revived in 2005 by Margaret Cook, a 1964 Northeastern
  graduate and Siberian Husky breeder at Teeco Kennels in Easton, Massachusetts.
- Three of Cook's huskies have held the crown since 2005.
- The current reigning live King Husky is named Moses. He visits campus for events,
  often accompanied by husky puppies.
- The Moses robot dog at Northeastern Vancouver is named for the reigning King Husky
  and acts as his robotic counterpart.
