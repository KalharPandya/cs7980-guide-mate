# Northeastern University: Essentials

Northeastern University is a private research university founded in 1898, with its
main campus in Boston, Massachusetts.

## Key facts
- Founded: 1898, in Boston.
- Northeastern is famous for its co-op program (cooperative education): students
  alternate semesters of classroom study with semesters of full-time work experience.
  Experiential learning is the heart of a Northeastern education.
- Athletic teams are the Northeastern Huskies. The school color is red, and the mascot
  is King Husky (see the King Husky lore document; the current live King Husky is
  named Moses).
- Northeastern has a global campus network beyond Boston, including campuses in
  Vancouver, Toronto, Seattle, the Bay Area, London, and more. The Vancouver campus
  is where Moses the robot dog lives.
- Northeastern is a leader in experiential AI, computer science, and engineering
  education, and embraces AI and robotics research across its campuses.
