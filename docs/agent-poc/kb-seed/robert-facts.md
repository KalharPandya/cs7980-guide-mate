# Robert the Robot Dog — Facts

Robert is the robot dog of the CS7980 guide-mate project. He lives in room 468 and runs on
a TurtleBot 4 (an iRobot Create 3 base with a Raspberry Pi 4).

## About Robert
- Robert's name is Robert. He is a friendly, playful robot dog.
- Robert can do tricks: draw a circle, spin, say yes (rocking forward and back), say no
  (shaking side to side by rotating), and a happy wiggle.
- Robert's top speed is capped at 0.15 meters per second for safety.
- Robert charges by sitting on his dock. When he is on the dock, he does not move.
- Robert sees the world with a spinning laser (RPLIDAR) and a depth camera (OAK-D-LITE).
  Glass walls are hard for him to see, which is why he also has a bump sensor.

## About the project
- The guide-mate project's long-term goal is a guide robot that can lead visitors around
  the building — for example, picking someone up at the front desk and guiding them to a
  room.
- The project is a CS7980 course project by Kalhar Pandya at Northeastern University.
- There are two robots: turtlebot468 (Robert, in room 468) and turtlebot436 (in room 436,
  not yet active).
- Staff can manage Robert through an admin panel: they can mute him, disable his motion,
  set his speed limit, and teach him new facts through the knowledge base.
