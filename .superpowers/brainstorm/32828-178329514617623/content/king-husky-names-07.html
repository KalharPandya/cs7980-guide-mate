Northeastern King Husky-inspired names

Names drawn from the King Husky story: the 1927 arrival, Sapsut/King Husky I, Seppala, the serum-run lineage, North Station, the good-luck statue, and the modern live mascot tradition.

Sapsut

The original husky name before he was presented as King Husky. Distinctive, real, and rooted.

My recommendation from this source: Sepp if you want a usable character name with expert-guide heritage; Sapsut if you want the dedication to be unmistakable and historically deep. Avoid using King Husky, Paws, or Moses as the main name unless the project has explicit university approval.
