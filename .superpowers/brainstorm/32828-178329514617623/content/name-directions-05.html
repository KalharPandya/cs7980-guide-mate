Name directions for the central AI concierge

Avoiding robot-first names. These should fit a central agent that can answer, route, dispatch, escalate, and later control many tools. Click names you like, or tell me what sounds wrong.

recommended / grounded expert

Concierge

A plain-English product name. It immediately tells users this AI can help, arrange, and route.

Map Knowledge Robot Staff

A. Concierge

Strongest concept fit, but generic. Could become "Campus Concierge" or "Concierge AI" in copy.

short / welcoming / navigational

Navi

Friendly and memorable. Reads as navigation plus assistant, without making the robot the brand.

Ask Route Voice Companion

B. Navi

Most approachable. Slightly less "expert concierge" unless paired with confident UI language.

knowledge + place intelligence

Atlas

Signals maps, campus knowledge, and spatial intelligence. Feels more premium and expandable.

Places Docs Routes Robots

C. Atlas

Best if the system's intelligence is about place, documents, and routing. Less explicitly service-oriented.

accessible / campus movement

Waypoint

A calm guidance name. Works for routes, check-ins, approvals, and handoffs.

Start Route Arrive Help

D. Waypoint

Very usable and clear, but more wayfinding app than central AI arsenal.

safety / accessibility / care

Haven

Soft, safe, accessible. Good for a support-oriented agent, less strong for multi-tool orchestration.

Safe Readable Human help

E. Haven

Warmest. I would avoid it if you want the agent to feel expert and operational.

orchestration / dispatch layer

Relay

Fast, technical, multi-system. The agent connects people, robots, knowledge, and staff.

Dispatch Status Ack Audit

F. Relay

Best for admin/operator identity. Probably too technical for the public-facing concierge.

My shortlist: Atlas if you want the product to feel like a place-intelligence platform; Navi if you want it to feel personal and easy; Concierge if concept clarity matters more than uniqueness. A hybrid could be: product name Atlas, role line "your campus concierge AI".
